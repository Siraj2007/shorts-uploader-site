Siraj Shorts Uploader

A personal tool for uploading short videos to TikTok accounts owned by SM.CO.

p>

What the app does

  • Lets the owner sign in to their own TikTok accounts through TikTok Login Kit.
  • Uploads the owner's own short videos to those accounts using the Content Posting API.
  • Checks the status of each upload and reports the result back to the owner.

The app is used only by its owner. It does not serve other users, and it does not post content on anyone else's behalf.

Data handling

  • Access tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer and are not shared with third parties.
  • Videos are sent only to TikTok, and only when the owner starts an upload.
  • No analytics, advertising, or data sales.

Privacy policy

This app collects no personal data beyond what TikTok returns when the owner signs in. Data stays on the owner's device and goes to TikTok only for uploads the owner asks for. Contact: sirajmunir.muzammil@gmail.coma>.
